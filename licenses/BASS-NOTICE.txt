BASS / BASSmix (un4seen developments)
=====================================

WDS Editor ships BASS and BASSmix shared libraries for audio playback and
mixing (for example libbass.dylib / bass.dll).

License summary (see https://www.un4seen.com for the authoritative terms):

  BASS is free for non-commercial use. If you are a non-commercial entity
  (e.g. an individual) and you are not making any money from your product
  (through sales, advertising, etc.), you may use BASS for free. Commercial
  use requires a paid licence from un4seen, on a per-platform basis.

  Redistribution of the BASS DLLs with your own end-user product is
  permitted under those terms. Reselling or sublicensing BASS itself is not.

WDS Editor is distributed as free, non-commercial software. If that changes,
obtain the appropriate commercial BASS licences before shipping.
