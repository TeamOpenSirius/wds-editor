Qt 6 — LGPL v3 compliance notes for WDS Editor
==============================================

WDS Editor links Qt 6 dynamically (shared libraries / frameworks). Qt is
used under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3 (or later), as
offered by The Qt Company for open-source use.

Corresponding source
  Obtain the Qt sources matching the version shown in Help → About
  (QT_VERSION_STR) from:
    https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/
    https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/
  or any mirror that publishes the same versioned source archives.

Relinking / replacing Qt
  Because Qt is dynamically linked, you may replace the bundled Qt shared
  libraries (or macOS frameworks) with a compatible build of the same major
  version, subject to the LGPL. On macOS they live under
  WDS Editor.app/Contents/MacOS/lib/ (QtCore, QtGui, QtWidgets, QtSvg,
  QtNetwork, …) and plugins under …/lib/plugins/.
